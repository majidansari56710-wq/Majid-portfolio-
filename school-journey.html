🎓 My Education Journey

📖 2010 – 2015 | Janiya Islamia Madersha

📍 Bhokraha Narsingh Rural Municipality-4, Sunsari, Nepal

This was the beginning of my educational journey. I studied from Grade 1 to Grade 5 and built a strong foundation in Islamic studies, discipline, moral values, and basic academic education. These five years shaped my character and inspired my passion for learning.

📚 2015 – 2017 | Darul Falah Madersha

📍 Bhokraha Narsingh Rural Municipality-6, Sunsari, Nepal

I continued my education here from Grade 5 to Grade 7. During these years, I strengthened my Islamic knowledge, discipline, confidence, and personal growth while continuing my academic journey.

🏫 2017 – 2024 | Shree Prithivi Narayan Secondary School

📍 Bhokraha Narsingh Rural Municipality-4, Sunsari, Nepal

I spent four unforgettable years at this school and completed my secondary education in the Nepali medium. My optional subjects were Economics and Education.

These were the best years of my school life. I made amazing friends, learned valuable lessons, and created memories that I will always cherish. One of the most exciting moments was our school tour to Pokhara, where we visited Phewa Lake, Davis Falls, Gupteshwor Cave, the World Peace Pagoda, Bindhyabasini Temple, and many other beautiful places. Every classroom, every event, every sports day, and every single day was memorable.
